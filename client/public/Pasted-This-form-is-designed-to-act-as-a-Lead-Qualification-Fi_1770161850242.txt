This form is designed to act as a Lead Qualification Filter. For a Phase 1 client (The "Foundation" stage), the goal is to identify if they are ready for institutional growth or if they first need the 700 Credit Club restoration.

By asking these critical questions, you move from being a "service provider" to a Consultant-Architect who is diagnosing their business health before the call even begins.

The "Sovereign Founder" Qualification Form
This structure ensures you capture the beginners (Phase 0-1) while flagging high-value Phase 2-3 clients who need the more complex tax and accounting categories you've identified.

1. The Identity Phase (Basics)
Full Name & Company Name (If applicable).

Best Contact Method (Email/Phone).

Current Venture Status: * I have a vision but no entity yet (Phase 0)

I am a new founder (0-12 months) (Phase 1)

I am an established business owner (Phase 2+)

2. The Critical Diagnosis (Pillar 1 Focus)
Credit Health: "On a scale of 1-10, how confident are you in your current borrowing power to fund this vision?"

Entity Status: "Do you currently have a registered EIN and a business bank account?"

The 90-Day Goal: "What is the single biggest bottleneck preventing you from scaling right now?" (Dropdown: Personal Credit, Legal Structure, Tax Compliance, or Lack of Capital).

3. Strategic Category Selection
"Which areas of your back-office require immediate hygiene?" (Checkboxes based on your new categories):

New Entity Formation & Structuring

Small Business Accounting (Bookkeeping/P&L)

Crypto Tax Accounting & Existing Entity Restructuring

Compliance Taxes (Payroll, Employment, Sales & Use)

Transaction & Non-Income Taxes (SALT)

4. Financial Sovereignty Check
Monthly Revenue Range: (Helps you pivot them to the $3,000 Suite vs. DIY Credit Repair).

Investment Readiness: "Are you prepared to invest in the professional foundation required for institutional-grade funding?"
